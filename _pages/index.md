# Welcome to TheRedP4nther's Cave! 🐾

## About Me

Hi, I'm **Lois del Río Somoza**, known online as **TheRedP4nther**. I'm a penetration tester and bug bounty hunter focused on **Web & API security**, especially **access control and authentication flaws**, with hands-on experience in **Active Directory** and **Windows/Linux privilege escalation**.

I'm **OSCP+**, **eJPT** and **PT1** certified. As a verified **[Bugcrowd](https://bugcrowd.com/h/theredp4nther)** researcher, I've had **35+ vulnerabilities validated across 11 programs** (8 public, 3 private), including **Volkswagen, NASA, the U.S. Department of Commerce, the U.S. EPA, Unisys, Linktree and Arlo**. Most of my findings involve broken access control (IDOR/BOLA), authentication bypass, federated identity (OAuth, MSAL, Keycloak) and sensitive data exposure.

I've also solved **200+ [Hack The Box](https://profile.hackthebox.com/profile/019f56fb-aa41-738b-988b-3b1cf4b54f5e) machines** and publish writeups for many of them here.
